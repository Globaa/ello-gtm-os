## Owner (economic buyer)
| | |
|---|---|
| Job to be done | Answer every customer who is ready to buy, and capture the order or booking, even while busy or closed. "Ello answers their questions, collects payments, schedules bookings and takes orders, even when you're busy or off." [co] |
| Pain | "Small businesses rarely lose customers on price. They lose them on timing. A message seen too late. A missed call. A question asked after hours." [vo] / "They were ready to buy. You were serving someone else." [vo] |
| Gain | Fewer missed orders and more bookings, per vertical: "Fewer missed orders. Less repetitive customer service." (food); "More bookings without spending the day on the phone." (salons) [cu]. One contact point instead of "scattered phone numbers, inboxes, and messages" [co] |
| Proof | Free plan at "₦0 /month" with "AI chat and voice through your Ello link"; Starter "₦15,000 /month" [co]. Customer service reps in Lagos are typically advertised at NGN 70,000 to 150,000 a month [cu]. "Turn the agent on when you need help, or answer customers yourself." [co] |

## Customer service or order-taking rep (champion)
| | |
|---|---|
| Job to be done | Handle customer enquiries and orders across channels without missing any. INFERRED from Lagos listings hiring reps "for order handling" [cu] |
| Pain | Repeated routine questions and scattered channels: "Take orders without taking every call." (food) [cu]; "Spend less time collecting information and more time doing the work." (home services) [cu] |
| Gain | The agent handles routine questions and passes the rest on: "Answer routine questions, capture orders and bookings, and hand conversations to your team when they need a person." [co]. Starter adds "Customer history and handover" and "2 team members" [co] |
| Proof | "Answers use your business information, with sensitive or complex questions passed to a person." [co]. Growth plan: "5 team members", "Conversion and team performance reporting" [co] |

## Social and ads runner (influencer, hypothesis)
| | |
|---|---|
| Job to be done | Turn Instagram and TikTok traffic into conversations and sales. "Turn social traffic into conversations, and conversations into customers." [cu] |
| Pain | Customers reach the business across "Instagram", "WhatsApp", "Phone", "Website", "Email", "DMs" [co]. Over half of 250 surveyed Nigerian e-commerce MSMEs sell only via Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp (GSMA 2023) [mk] |
| Gain | One link for every placement: "Instagram bio", "TikTok bio", "Google profile", "Website", "Business card", "QR code", "Storefront", "Ads" [co]. "No. They tap your link and the call or chat starts in their browser" [vo] |
| Proof | Handle format "ello.africa/@yourbusinessname" [co]. Ello calls run "over the internet, with no phone number and without using the public telephone network" [co] |
