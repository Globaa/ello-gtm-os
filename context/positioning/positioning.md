## Positioning statement
For small businesses in Nigeria that sell through Instagram, WhatsApp, phone calls and their storefront, Ello is the contact layer that gives the business one memorable handle customers can call or chat from their browser, with no app and no phone number. When the owner can't answer, Ello's AI agent uses the business's own catalogue and opening hours to answer questions, take orders and book appointments, then hands anything sensitive to a person. Unlike AI chat tools that live inside WhatsApp and Instagram, or AI phone agents that need a phone line, Ello puts calls, chat and AI behind one link, keeps the owner's personal number private, and starts free.

## Watch-outs
- "African SMEs" is a headline claim; only Nigeria is evidenced (naira pricing, Nigeria focus) [co].
- Calls need the customer to have internet; VoiceAgents works "with or without internet" [cm].
- Do not position on payments until the terms/pricing conflict is resolved [co].
