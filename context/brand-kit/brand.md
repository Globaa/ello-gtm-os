## Tone of voice
Style observations are marked INFERRED in the research file; each is drawn from the quoted copy.
| Trait | What it looks like | Example from Ello |
|---|---|---|
| Short and rhythmic | Two-beat or three-beat lines | "Call it. Chat it. Ask it." / "Three steps. That's it." |
| Speaks to the owner | Second person, "your business", "your customers" | "Your business. Ready to respond." |
| Plain words | No "omnichannel", "CX" or "LLM"; AI is a helper, not the headline | "Help when you're busy" |
| Customer-first benefit | Frames value as not missing customers, not as saving time or money | "They were ready to buy. You were serving someone else." |
| Calm, low hype | No stats, no superlatives, almost no exclamation marks | "Free to start. Most businesses are set up in one sitting." |
| Local, standard English | Naira prices and Lagos places in demos; no Pidgin on the site | "It's ₦8,500." / "Yaba." |
| Reassuring and direct | FAQ answers start with "No." and explain in one line | "No. Ello runs in the browser, and on the Ello app if you want calls to ring your phone." |

## Rules for our copy
- No em dashes (repo rule).
- Write prices as Ello does: "₦15,000 /month" style, naira first [co].
- Match Ello's FAQ framing for objections; questions Ello lists: "Do I need a new phone or SIM?", "Do my customers have to download an app?", "What happens when I'm busy or asleep?", "I'd rather answer customers myself." [vo]
