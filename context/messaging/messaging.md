# Ello Messaging

Status: DRAFT, 2026-11-13. Sources: research files only, codes [co] company, [cu] customers, [cm] competitors, [mk] market, [vo] voice. Quotes are Ello's own words. No customer proof exists yet [cu]: proof points are product facts.

## Core message
**One link for every customer conversation. Customers call or chat your business from their browser, and when you can't answer, Ello can.**
Built from: "Ello: one handle for every customer conversation", "One business link. Calls, chat, and AI. No app download for customers.", "When you can't answer, Ello can." [vo]
