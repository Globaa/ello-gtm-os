## Banned
### Banned angles
- Replacing the hire, or replacing staff. The prospect just decided to hire; Ello works alongside the person they hire [workflow-spec Section 4; messaging.md].
- Anything built on the posted salary.
- Diagnosing the business as losing customers or failing (insult test).
- Payments ("collect payments", "get paid through Ello"): terms say "Ello does not process payments"; conflict unresolved [company.md].
- Local languages (Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo): Not found [company.md].
- "Across Africa" or any country beyond Nigeria.
- Compliance claims ("NDPA compliant").

### Banned words (company-specific)
- "WhatsApp integration", "omnichannel", "CX", "LLM", "trusted by".

### Never in copy
- hi@ello.africa or any internal data.

## Voice notes
- Register: plain, calm, second person, short lines, no hype, no exclamation marks [brand.md].
- Hedges about the prospect: "my guess is", "could be wrong", "probably".
- Subjects: lowercase, 3-5 words, same subject across the thread.
