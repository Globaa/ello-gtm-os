# Ello: Buying Signals Research (raw)

Checked: 2026-11-12. Raw research, not conclusions. Public evidence of what makes a business need Ello now. Ello publishes no customer data, so most signals are INFERRED from Ello's own pain statements plus public market evidence. Each signal shows the evidence and how it could be detected.

## Ello's own trigger language (what they say the pain is)
- "A message seen too late. A missed call. A question asked after hours." (https://ello.africa/about)
- "They were ready to buy. You were serving someone else." (https://ello.africa/)
- "Your business needs a contact point. Not another phone." (https://ello.africa/)
- "Turn your Instagram bio into a business desk." (https://ello.africa/use-cases)
- "Never lose a property lead to a missed call." (https://ello.africa/use-cases)
All checked 2026-11-12.

## Deadlines and timing hooks
- 1 Oct 2026: WhatsApp Business Platform starts charging for certain customer replies (sources in market.md).
- Sept 2026 onward: Meta One paid plans, Business Agent moving to paid (sources in market.md).
- 30 June 2026: CAC annual returns deadline; July 2026 strike-off notice for about 100,000 companies (sources in market.md).
- Seasonal peaks (Black Friday, December/Detty December, Valentine's, Easter, Sallah): Not researched. INFERRED: enquiry spikes for food, beauty and retail.

## Funding / new leader / expansion signals
- Ello's buyers are micro and small businesses; funding rounds and executive hires are rare and mostly not public (INFERRED). Expansion signals that fit: second branch opening, moving from Instagram-only to a physical store, new delivery city. Evidence: Not found (no study); detection via social posts and Google Business Profile.
