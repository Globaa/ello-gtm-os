## Style observations (INFERRED)
- Short sentences, often two-beat pairs ("Call it. Chat it. Ask it."; "Not another phone.").
- Second person ("your business", "your customers"); speaks to the owner, not a manager.
- Plain words; no jargon like "omnichannel", "CX", "LLM". "AI" appears, but the product is framed as the business's contact point, not as AI.
- Benefit framed as not missing customers, rather than as saving time or money.
- Naira prices in demo chats; light Nigerian context ("Yaba") but standard English, no Pidgin on the site.
- Calm, low-hype tone: no stats, no superlatives, no exclamation marks except one demo line ("Yes! We're open until 10pm.").
- No em dashes seen in the quoted copy.
