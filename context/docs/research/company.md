### Conflict: payments
- Pricing and homepage say Ello takes payments ("collects payments"; "2% on payments you take through Ello").
- Terms (last updated "August 23, 2026") say: "During this phase, orders are captured for the business to fulfil. Ello does not process payments." and "Orders placed today are capture-only; do not charge customers through Ello." (https://ello.africa/terms, checked 2026-11-12)
- Privacy (last updated "October 3, 2026") names Paystack for "Taking payments and paying businesses out."
- INFERRED: payments were added after the terms were written, and the terms are out of date. Confirm with the team.
