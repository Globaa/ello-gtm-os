## Detail

### Zana AI
- URLs: https://usezanaai.com/, https://usezanaai.com/pricing (fetched, checked 2026-11-12)
- Headline: "Your business never goes offline again."
- Subheadline opens: "Zana AI helps Nigerian businesses automate WhatsApp and Instagram conversations..."
- Channels: WhatsApp and Instagram DMs now; Messenger, Telegram, LinkedIn, "SMS (via Termii)" listed as "Coming soon". No voice, no web chat.
- Languages: English, Nigerian Pidgin, or a mix (paraphrase).
- Pricing: Starter ₦15,000 (WhatsApp only, 2,000 AI replies); Growth ₦35,000 (WhatsApp + Instagram, 10,000 replies); Agency ₦85,000 (50,000 replies). 3-day trial. Paystack or bank transfer.
- Claims: "200+ Businesses on zana"; "15s" average AI response time; "₦0 Per message cost".
- Named testimonials: Adaeze Okonkwo (fashion boutique, Lagos), Kemi Adeleke (skincare brand, Abuja), Emeka Bassey (electronics reseller, Port Harcourt).
- vs Ello (INFERRED): same starter price (₦15,000) as Ello Starter; Zana lives inside WhatsApp/Instagram, Ello gives the business its own link plus voice.

### CallAI (CherryBrooks LLC)
- URLs: https://gocallai.ng/, https://gocallai.ng/pricing (fetched, checked 2026-11-12)
- Headline: "Your business calls, answered."
- Subheadline: "CallAI Standard gives you one or two AI voice agents that answer your line and call customers back."
- Channels: inbound and outbound voice; WhatsApp Business API inbox, SMS, email, Facebook/Instagram/LinkedIn posting and replies.
- Pricing (ex-VAT): Starter ₦75,000/month (100 min, 1 number); Growth ₦150,000 (220 min); Business ₦300,000 (500 min). Enterprise from ₦3,500,000 setup + ₦2,100,000/year.
- Claims: "NDPA certified"; SOC 2 Type II; ISO 27001; "50+ languages"; "Setup Time 24 hours".
- Named customers: Nile University of Nigeria, African Import and Export Solutions, AfriproEdu, Woy Travels.
- vs Ello (INFERRED): CallAI is 5x Ello Starter's price and aimed at SMEs with a phone line and budget; Ello needs no phone number.

### Obeks AI
- URLs: https://obeks.com.ng/, https://obeks.com.ng/pricing.html (fetched, checked 2026-11-12)
- Headline: "Hire AI staff for your business."
- Subheadline: "Answers customers, takes orders and books appointments on WhatsApp and web. Live in 60 seconds, no code."
- Languages: "African Pidgin, Yoruba-influenced English, Hausa-influenced English and more."
- Pricing: Starter ₦5,000/month (1-month free trial, 1,000 AI conversations); Business ₦20,000/month; Enterprise custom.
- Partners: "Official Meta Tech Provider".
- Named customers: CIAMO Hotel, Spectrum Microfinance Bank, Lagos Music Festivals, Landman Solutions, Naija Trainings, others.
- Has a 60% reseller/partner cut (paraphrase).

### VoiceAgents (EqualyzAI)
- URL: https://usevoiceagent.ai/ (fetched, checked 2026-11-12)
- Headline: "Speak to Your Customers in Yoruba – and Never Miss a Sale Again." Section: "Built for the Way Nigerian Businesses Actually Run."
- Channels: phone calls only, over the normal phone network "with or without internet".
- Languages: Yoruba, Igbo, Pidgin, Hausa, Nigerian English.
- Pricing (USD): Starter $12.50/month (150 min); Growth $40.80 (500 min); Business $165.80 (2,000 min).
- vs Ello (INFERRED): VoiceAgents leads on local languages and PSTN reach; Ello's calls need internet (browser or app). Language support is a gap Ello has not addressed publicly.

### NativeTalk
- URLs: https://nativetalk.io/, https://nativetalk.io/pricing (fetched, checked 2026-11-12)

- Claims: "6,000+" businesses; "1 Billion+" minutes of calls. Brands named: MultiChoice, OPay, Oraimo, SportyBet, Pizza Hut.

- Meta One business plans: Essential $14.99/month (verification, impersonation protection, Business Agent on WhatsApp); Advanced $49.99/month (https://petapixel.com/2026/09/15/meta-adds-more-subscription-offerings-in-the-form-of-meta-one/, via web search, checked 2026-11-12).
- Nigeria: WhatsApp ran a "Power Your Hustle with WhatsApp Business" campaign with a masterclass covering the Meta Business Agent (https://brandspurng.com/2026/09/07/whatsapp-spotlights-nigerian-small-businesses-in-power-your-hustle-with-whatsapp-business-campaign/, via web search, checked 2026-11-12). A formal Nigeria launch: Not found.
- INFERRED: the biggest substitute threat; it sits where Nigerian SMEs already sell. Ello's counter is the channel-neutral handle, voice, and verification.
