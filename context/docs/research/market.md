# Ello: Market Research (raw)

Checked: 2026-11-12. Raw research, not conclusions. Regulation and market changes that may create urgency for Ello's buyers (Nigerian small businesses). Most items come from web search summaries of news and law-firm articles; the original documents were not read unless stated. INFERRED = my inference.

## 1. WhatsApp Business Platform charges for customer replies (from 1 Oct 2026)
- Meta switched WhatsApp Business Platform billing from per-conversation to per-message on 1 July 2025 (https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing, via web search, checked 2026-11-12).
- Reports say Meta began charging businesses for certain customer replies on the WhatsApp Business Platform from 1 October 2026; reported Nigeria rates about $0.0101 per utility message and about $0.062 per marketing message (https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/technology/1727481-whatsapp-charge-nigerian-businesses-message-october-1-what-companies-know/ and https://msmeafricaonline.com/whatsapp-to-charge-businesses-for-customer-replies-from-october-2026/, via web search, checked 2026-11-12).
- Applies to the API, not the free WhatsApp Business app (same sources).
- DUBAWA fact-check: the "N14 per message" claim is misleading for ordinary users (https://dubawa.org/misleading-whatsapp-not-set-to-charge-nigerians-n14-per-message/, via web search).
- INFERRED: raises costs for WhatsApp-API chatbots (Zana, Obeks, CallAI); Ello's own link avoids per-message Meta fees. Verify current Meta rate card before using in copy.

## 2. Meta moving business AI behind a paywall (2026)
- Meta Business Agent: free to start, paid subscriptions to follow (https://www.fonearena.com/blog/484218/meta-business-agent-platform-features.html, via web search).
- Meta One business plans: Essential $14.99/month, Advanced $49.99/month (https://petapixel.com/2026/09/15/meta-adds-more-subscription-offerings-in-the-form-of-meta-one/, via web search, checked 2026-11-12).
- INFERRED: SMEs who relied on free Meta tools now face a paid choice, which opens a comparison moment.

## 3. Telecom tariffs up 50% (Jan 2025)
- NCC approved a tariff increase capped at 50% on 20 January 2025, the first in about 11 years (https://techcabal.com/2025/01/20/ncc-approves-tariff-increase/, via web search, checked 2026-11-12).
- Reported new rates: minimum call rate ₦6.40 to ₦9.60 per minute; average call ₦11 to ₦16.50; SMS ₦4 to ₦6; data about ₦350/GB to ₦525/GB (https://nairametrics.com/2025/01/20/ncc-approves-50-tariff-adjustment-for-telecom-operators, via web search).
- INFERRED: Ello calls run over the internet, not the phone network ("without using the public telephone network", https://ello.africa/privacy). Cost argument cuts both ways: no call charges, but data costs also rose.

## 4. Data protection: NDPA 2023 and GAID 2025
- GAID 2025 took effect 19 September 2025; NDPR 2019 and its 2020 framework stopped applying (https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/privacy-protection/1606106/the-nigeria-data-protection-commission-issues-the-general-application-and-implementation-directive-2025-gaid, via web search, checked 2026-11-12).
- No blanket SME exemption in the NDPA or GAID (https://webiis10.mondaq.com/nigeria/data-protection/1689736/assessing-the-implications-of-the-gaid-implementation-for-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises-smes-in-nigeria, via web search).
- Annual compliance audit obligations; sources differ on whether they apply to small firms (https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/data-protection/1734060/what-you-should-know-about-the-new-data-protection-compliance-audit-regime-in-nigeria, via web search).
- Ello's privacy policy names the NDPA 2023 and NDPC, and says "we do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, we do not record call audio" (https://ello.africa/privacy, checked 2026-11-12).
- INFERRED: weak urgency for micro businesses (low enforcement), but a trust point. Do not claim Ello makes a business compliant.

## 5. CAC annual returns and strike-offs
- Annual returns deadline for many entities: 30 June 2026 (https://resource.scp-law.com/2026/06/11/the-june-30-deadline-is-closer-than-you-think-what-every-registered-business-must-do-now/, via web search, checked 2026-11-12).
- CAC notice of intention to strike off about 100,000 companies on 15 July 2026, sixth batch since 2023 (https://www.premiumtimesng.com/business/business-news/895765-cac-begins-sixth-batch-of-company-strike-off-targets-100000-companies.html, via web search).
- CAC removed more than 400,000 companies in 2025 (https://leadership.ng/cac-delists-400000-inactive-non-compliant-companies/, via web search).
- Link to Ello: verification requires a "registration certificate" (https://ello.africa/). INFERRED: registered, compliant businesses are Ello's eligible pool; CAC status is a usable list filter.

## 6. Social commerce is how Nigerian SMEs sell
- GSMA (2023): over half of 250 surveyed Nigerian e-commerce MSMEs relied only on Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp (https://www.gsma.com/mobilefordevelopment/resources/webinar-e-commerce-in-nigeria-unleashing-the-opportunity-for-msmes/, via web search).
- Meta-commissioned: 14 million Nigerian SMEs used Meta apps in 2025 (https://guardian.ng/technology/meta-injects-820m-into-nigerian-economy-boosts-81-of-businesses/, via web search; Meta-funded).
- BusinessDay cites a 2024 Social Commerce Nigeria Report: TikTok sellers grew revenue 2 to 4 times faster than Instagram-only sellers (secondary; original report not found).
- INFERRED: Ello's placement list ("Instagram bio", "TikTok bio", "Google profile") maps to this.

## 7. Market size
- NBS: 39,654,385 MSMEs (2020), 96.9% micro; SMEDAN DG repeated the figure in 2025 (https://www.thecable.ng/from-influence-to-income-nigerian-small-businesses-embrace-social-media-platforms-to-drive-growth, via web search).
- Some sources cite about 37 million (SMEDAN/NBS survey, year unclear) or 41 million (https://aibase.ng/ai-opportunity/viable-ai-startup-business-ideas-for-nigerians/, a blog). Figures conflict; use NBS 2020 with its date.

## 8. Missed-call / slow-reply cost
- No rigorous Nigeria study found quantifying lost sales from missed calls or slow replies (web search, checked 2026-11-12). Claims seen are vendor marketing (e.g. Gerbo: "80% of missed callers never ring back", https://gerbospeaks.netlify.app/blog/ai-call-answering-uk-small-business). Not found: independent data.
